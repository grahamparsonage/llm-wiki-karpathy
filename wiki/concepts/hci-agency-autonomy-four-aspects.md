# HCI Four Aspects of Agency and Autonomy

Organising framework from [[bennett-chi23-hci-agency-autonomy]] for mapping how HCI papers approach human agency and autonomy—usable when scoping literature or design claims.

---

## The four aspects

### 1. Self-causality and identity

| Sub-category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Executing** | Material involvement in tasks and outcomes |
| **Decision** | Choice over tasks, processes, outcomes |
| **Self-congruence** | Outcomes align with values/goals (may lack causal involvement) |
| **Self-construction** | Effects on identity, values, goals over time |

### 2. Experience and materiality

- **Material** — actual ability to effect outcomes, resources, constraints
- **Experiential** — felt sense-of-agency, sense-of-control

These can diverge (e.g., manipulated sense-of-agency without causal influence).

### 3. Time-scales

- **Micro-interaction** — seconds (button press, binding)
- **Episode** — up to hours (game session, study)
- **Life** — days and beyond (identity, chronic conditions, development)

### 4. Independence and interdependence

- **Independence** — acting/thinking without others
- **Interdependence** — group, social grounds of agency

Both may appear in the same paper.
