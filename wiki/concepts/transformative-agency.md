# Transformative Agency

Children's capacity to change personal, shared, or societal matters for the better—moving from critical knowledge to **sustained action**, not only reflection or design within a study.

---

## Definition

From [[iivari-idc24-transformative-agency]] (Cultural-Historical Activity Theory):

Ability to actively change one's own matters, shared matters, or societal matters towards a more just society—collectively enacted, not a fixed individual trait.

Linked to **expansive learning**: learners create novel practices/objects beyond stable curricula.

---

## Six observable manifestations

1. **Resisting** — opposing existing practices
2. **Criticising** — naming problems in current arrangements
3. **Explicating** — articulating possibilities for change
4. **Envisioning** — future-oriented proposals
5. **Committing to action** — concrete obligations
6. **Taking action** — real-world change beyond the classroom

---

## Emergence conditions

- Demanding situations with opportunity to analyse, envision, redesign activity collaboratively
- Tensions and conflicts as generative (not only obstacles)
- Design and making as transformative devices

---

## Gap noted in child–AI review

[[agency-lit-review-hal]]: transformative/critical agency appears mainly in **speculative workshops**; weak evidence of sustained praxis after studies end. [[iivari-idc24-transformative-agency]] addresses this gap for CE/PD but from Nordic school contexts.

---

## PD design implications

Supplement standard CE participatory design aims with six transformative aims (stance, explication, pathfinding, construction impact, argument/mobilisation, change-agent identity)—see source page for phase mapping.
