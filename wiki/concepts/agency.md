# Agency

Canonical wiki term for the capacity to act intentionally—making decisions and enacting them towards outcomes—in human–technology interaction, especially for children.

---

## Working definition (child-AI corpus)

From [[agency-lit-review-hal]]: *the ability to act intentionally to make decisions and enact them in order to achieve an outcome.*

Related terms often used interchangeably in HCI unless distinguished:

| Term | Typical emphasis |
|---|---|
| **Autonomy** | Self-governance, independence, alignment with values |
| **Empowerment** | Capacity-building; skills and reflexivity to engage critically with technology |
| **Agentic / agentic capacity** | Adjective form; developmental or relational enactment |

See [[glossary]] for usage rules.

---

## HCI field status

[[bennett-chi23-hci-agency-autonomy]] finds agency and autonomy operate as **umbrella constructs** in general HCI—broad, inconsistently defined, rarely operationalised.

In child–AI HCI specifically ([[agency-lit-review-hal]]):

- Rarely explicitly defined per paper
- Conceptualised as innate, threatened, or developable
- Observed through choice, control, critique—not standardised metrics

## Forms relevant to child–AI design

| Form | Description | Example evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Over self | Planning, self-regulation | Video-watching plans, transitions |
| Over system | Opposed control of AI outputs | Rejecting copilot suggestions |
| Over environment | Critique/redesign of status quo | Speculative co-design, institutional challenge |
| Transformative | Sustained change of self/shared/societal matters | [[transformative-agency]] |

---

## Enabling vs constraining conditions (synthesis)

**Enablers**: AI/data literacy, meaningful choice with visible consequences, participatory design, self-efficacy, scaffolded support

**Constraints**: System opacity, volition displacement, surveillance/power asymmetry, choice overload, deterministic pedagogy, resignation about data permanence

Design implication: agency is not maximised by unlimited choice; requires balance of support and control ([[agency-lit-review-hal]], Brod).

## Analytical lens for documentation

When documenting features or research, specify:

1. Which **form** of agency is in scope
2. Which of Bennett's **[[hci-agency-autonomy-four-aspects]]** apply
3. Whether framing is **individual** or **[[relational-agency-lens]]**
4. Time-scale: moment, session, or developmental trajectory
