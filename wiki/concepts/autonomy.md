# Autonomy

Self-governance: acting in accordance with one's own values, goals, and laws of conduct. In HCI often entangled with [[agency]].

---

## Etymology vs agency

From [[bennett-chi23-hci-agency-autonomy]]:

- **Autonomy** (*autos* + *nomos*): self-governance
- **Agency** (*agere*): to set in motion, perform—self-causality

HCI literature frequently treats them as synonyms; child–AI review uses collective label "agency" unless distinguishing deliberately.

---

## Child–AI specific usage

- **Data autonomy** — sense of control over personal data on platforms (Wang et al.)
- **Digital autonomy** — design patterns for children's independence online
- **Developmentally aligned** design — autonomy matched to developmental stage

Tension: full autonomy vs safety for children requires **balance**, not binary enable/disable ([[agency-lit-review-hal]], Bennett corpus).
