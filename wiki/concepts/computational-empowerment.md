# Computational Empowerment

CCI paradigm for engaging children constructively, analytically, and critically with technology's effects on their lives and society—not only learning how computers work.

---

## Definition

From Iversen, Smith & Dindler (2018), via [[ijcci-computational-empowerment-editorial]]:

Empowering children and young people to make **critical and informed decisions** about technology's role in their lives and their potential to **shape** that role.

---

## vs Computational Thinking

| Computational Thinking | Computational Empowerment |
|---|---|
| Understand computing concepts | Critique, construct, and reshape socio-technical futures |
| Often decontextualised skills | Humanistic, political, participatory |
| Dominant in curricula | Maturing CCI research agenda |

---

## Four components (Iivari et al. 2024 extension)

1. **Critical inquiry** — understanding
2. **Critical design** — construction
3. **Reimagining the future** — speculative/not-yet-real
4. **[[transformative-agency]]** — action and sustained change (fourth, previously underdeveloped)

---

## Research agenda levels (Smith et al., 2023)

- Participatory Practice
- Educational Principles
- Digital Tools (e.g., DORIT analytical model for values/intentionality)

---

## Critical questions for writers

- Who gets empowered to do what?
- How do power relationships shift?
- Is impact sustainable beyond a single workshop or semester?
- Can CE scale through policy without diluting critical stance?
