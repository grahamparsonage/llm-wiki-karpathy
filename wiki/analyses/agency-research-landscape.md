## Convergent findings

1. **Definitional ambiguity** is systemic—not unique to child–AI work
2. **Agency is plural**: individual control, critical redesign, and collective transformation are different objects of study
3. **Empowerment ≠ unlimited choice**; scaffolding and literacy enable agency
4. **Social distribution** matters; single-user UX framing is insufficient
5. **Evaluation gap**: observation-rich, metrics-poor; need targeted instruments per agency form

## Tensions to flag in writing

| Tension | Resolution direction |
|---|---|
| Child agency vs system autonomy | Collaborative/negotiated control; not zero-sum |
| Safety vs autonomy | Configurable, child-centred overrides; transparency |
| Speculative critique vs real change | Longitudinal PD; transformative agency aims |
| Western/Nordic PD values vs Global South | Cultural translation; career vs civic empowerment |
| Agency as innate vs cultivated | Age-appropriate scaffolding; explicit definitions per study |
