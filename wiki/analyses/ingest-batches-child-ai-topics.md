## Batch 1 (Bai, Bennetot, Chowdhury, Choi, Chubb)

| Core topic | Papers | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Participatory & speculative design** | Bai2023 | Children as co-designers of future AI; PD underused for child-relevant systems |
| **Help-seeking & control** | Bennetot2020 | When AI offers help vs child requests it shapes learning and agency |
| **Recommender harm / Goldilocks** | Chowdhury2023 | Collaborative filtering undermines child agency, curiosity, self-concept |
| **Co-creative (not tutoring) AI** | Choi2026 | Tangible + voice co-storytelling; scaffolding without displacing child authorship |
