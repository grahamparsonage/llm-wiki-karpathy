## Key findings

- ~3,000 languages at risk by end of century; early **language appreciation** (not only communication) aids preservation
- Generative AI for bilingual storybooks, culturally specific illustrations (DALL·E, Midjourney), VR immersive cultural contexts
- **Co-create/co-design** with children alongside AI to support **agency** in language learning
- Bridge traditional teaching (textbooks, poetry) with personalised AI recommendations
- Ethical risks: accuracy, bias, cultural appropriation; LLMs must not invent non-historical language forms—oversight needed
- KidsTeam co-design acknowledged

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Links **computational empowerment** and cultural identity to child–AI **co-creation**; ethical guardrails for generative tools affecting heritage languages.
