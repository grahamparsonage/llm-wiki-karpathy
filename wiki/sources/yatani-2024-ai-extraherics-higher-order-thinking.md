# AI as Extraherics

Conceptual framework: **extraheric AI** prompts questions and alternative perspectives instead of direct answers, to sustain higher-order thinking under cognitive load theory and Bloom's taxonomy.

## Key findings

- Over-reliance on generative AI risks **deskilling**, uncritical acceptance of hallucinations, reduced ownership and accomplishment
- Typical human–AI design replaces/augments cognition (orthotics/prosthetics); **extraheric AI** increases **germane cognitive load** to foster creativity, critical thinking, problem-solving
- Contrasts with intelligent tutoring systems (education-specific) and effortful UIs (intrinsic load for system learning)
- Interaction strategies and evaluation methods proposed for cognitive, attitudinal, behavioural change
- Applicable beyond classrooms—including children's misinformation literacy and reflective engagement with AI outputs

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Counterweight to copilot-style child–AI that answers for children; supports **computational empowerment** and critical agency vs passive consumption of AI-generated content.
