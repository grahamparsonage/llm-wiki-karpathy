# Autiverse — Autistic Adolescents' Daily Narratives

CHI 2026: tablet app with peer-like AI, ABC model, and four-panel comic visualisation; two-week deployment (N=10 dyads).

## Key findings

- Text-centric journaling burdens executive function; **Autiverse** uses conversation + token-based comic strip co-editing
- Customisable **AI peer** elicits antecedent–behaviour–consequence + emotion; stepwise questioning for missing narrative parts
- Deployment: scaffolded coherent narratives; parents learned new event/emotion details; used comics as home conversation bridges
- Adolescents reported enjoyment, comfortable disclosure, strong sense of **agency**; parent nearby in safeguard role
- Design implications: adaptive scaffolding across autism profiles, socio-emotionally appropriate AI peers, balance adolescent autonomy with parental involvement

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Exemplifies **co-agency** and expressive support for neurodivergent youth; AI as facilitator of child-led narrative, not instructor replacing self-expression.
