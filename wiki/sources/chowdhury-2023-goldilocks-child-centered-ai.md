# Towards Goldilocks Zone in Child-centered AI

Position paper using YouTube Kids to argue for value-driven, age-appropriate child–AI interaction—not reactive safeguarding only.

## Core argument

Collaborative-filtering recommenders (clicks + watch time) undermine child **agency**, self-concept, curiosity, and creativity by surfacing demographic-trend content and enabling passive drift.

---

## Design recommendations

1. Clear **accountability** for algorithmic harms (platform + creator + parent roles)
2. **Co-design with children and parents** for cultural fairness
3. Avoid collaborative recommendation for child-centric experiences; age-appropriate cognitive design
4. Need diverse child data with **privacy-preserving** collection
