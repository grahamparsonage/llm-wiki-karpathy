# Should Artificial Agents Ask for Help?

Compares child and Q-learning learning on Towers of Hanoi when expert help is canonical vs player-requested.

## Key findings

- Expert intervention (canonical or on request) can accelerate task learning for **children and Q-learning** similarly
- Children communicate uncertainty early but do not always help-seek — intrinsic/extrinsic motivation complexity
- Links developmental robotics, selective social learning, collaborative problem-solving

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

**Agency over system:** when to request vs receive unsolicited AI assistance; design implication for copilots and tutors that offer help without displacing child initiative.
