# The Future of Child Development in the AI Era

Expert consultation (n=15) plus literature review on how GenAI and ambient AI reshape children's cognitive and socio-emotional development (0–25 years).

## Key findings

- Environment shapes neural development through ~age 25; AI in education/leisure alters developmental trajectories
- GenAI (post-ChatGPT 2022) accelerated public, conscious human–AI interaction vs hidden recommender-era AI
- Experts expect AI to transform leisure, education, and human–machine interaction—benefits and risks in **sensitive periods**
- Research lag: environmental change outpaces evidence on behavioural impact
- Flags biometrics/physiological monitoring, immersive VR/AR, and child-development AI monitoring
- Advocates international collaboration, child-centred regulation, stakeholder education (regulators, developers, parents, educators, children)

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Macro framing for **agency**, wellbeing, and policy: balances generational moral panic with unprecedented pace of change; supports evidence-based safeguards without assuming children are passive victims of technology.
