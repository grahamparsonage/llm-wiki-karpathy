CHI 2024 child-centred AI workshop proposal: UI/UX best practices and metrics for safe, trustworthy child–AI interaction.

## Key findings

- Workshop goal: collaborative framework for **measuring** safety, trustworthiness, reliability in child–AI UI/UX
- Proposed methods: co-design workshops, child-centric design sessions, rapid prototyping, mixed qualitative/quantitative evaluation
- UI/UX practices: simplified navigation, age-appropriate content, gamification, **bounded** customisation (caregiver override), transparency on AI limits and data use, content filtering, inclusive/fair design
- Metrics (Schneiderman HCAI): transparency, explainability, accuracy, consistency, robustness, fairness, **user control**
- Stakeholder involvement: child psychologists, educators, assessment specialists, children

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Connects **trust** and safety measurement to design practice; tension between child autonomy in UI and caregiver-mediated safety mirrors broader agency debates.
