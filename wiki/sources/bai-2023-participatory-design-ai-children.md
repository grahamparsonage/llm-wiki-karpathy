## Key findings

- **60 ideas** (ages 4–16): problems = helping people (52%), environment (17%), kindness (27%), connection, equality
- **AI applications imagined:** intelligent agents 71% (robots, voice, social); recommender apps; NLP; IoT
- **AI capabilities (K-12 Big Ideas):** perception, representation/reasoning, natural interaction common; gaps in learning-from-data and societal impact
- **Opportunities:** envisioning future AI; promoting AI literacy through authentic PD; informing educators on misconceptions (anthropomorphism, trust)
- **Challenges:** limited child AI technical knowledge; limited adult facilitation — need toolkits for inclusive child–adult AI PD

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Positions **participatory design** as underused for child-relevant AI systems; children as informants and low-fi designers, not only end users.
