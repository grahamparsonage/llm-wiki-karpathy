## Core definition (Iversen, Smith & Dindler, 2018)

**Computational Empowerment**: how children and young people can be empowered to make critical and informed decisions about the role of technology in their lives and society—and their potential role in shaping it.

Contrasts with **Computational Thinking**, which focuses on understanding computing concepts.

---

## Intellectual roots

- Heidi Schelhowe's *Bildung* / digital literacy (*Medienbildung*) — simultaneous change in self and world perception
- Scandinavian Participatory Design — empowerment, emancipation, democracy
- Critical design traditions — inclusion, liberation, anti-marginalisation
- Morales-Navarro & Kafai: co-creating computing futures by decoding and coding artefacts critically

---

## Open questions posed by the special issue

- Supporting teaching with digital and emerging technologies
- Multiple conceptualisations of CE
- Sustainable long-term impact across educational contexts
- Inclusion for underrepresented groups
- Critical question: *who gets empowered to do what*, and how power shifts
- Scaling CE through policy in diverse school settings
