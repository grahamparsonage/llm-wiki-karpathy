# Toward Children-Centric AI

Hermeneutic **growth model** for children–AI (ages 7–11): purpose of interaction is children's development; distinguish positive vs negative bias.

## Key findings

- Open AI co-creation spaces: sensitise to negative biases, appropriate top-down diversity values, inclusive mindset
- Critiques human-centric AI that prioritises machine purpose (do-no-harm) over child development
- Recursive ML systems can support growth if bias handled as preference not only discrimination
