# Tinker Tales

Tangible NFC storytelling board + voice conversational AI; narrative and SEL scaffolding for child–AI **co-creation** (not AI-led tutoring).

## System

- Physical board (Start / Journey / Climax / End), NFC story elements, mobile app, voice dialogue
- Scaffolding grounded in Applebee narrative development + CASEL social–emotional learning
- Study: N=10, within-subject structured vs generic AI scaffolding

---

## Findings (headline)

- Children treated AI as attentive collaborator; scaffolding supported coherent narratives **without reducing agency**
- RQs: scaffolding shapes interaction; children's perception of AI; design insights for co-creative child–AI

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Exemplar shift from **instructional AI** to **iterative co-creation**; balances automation with child-led narrative control.
