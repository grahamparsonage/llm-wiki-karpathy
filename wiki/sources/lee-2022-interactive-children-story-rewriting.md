# Interactive Children's Story Rewriting

Human–AI interface for parent–child collaborative story rewriting (ages 3–8); GPT-3 pipeline for entities, questions, and coherent rewrites.

## Key findings

- Story rewriting supports **autonomy**, **competence**, and **effectance** (local/global narrative impact) in interactive storytelling
- Parents face high cognitive load guiding rewrites for pre-readers; AI offloads entity extraction, question generation, and text generation
- Three design goals: (1) candidate story dimensions to change; (2) parent–child Q&A for relationship-building (dialogic reading); (3) rewritten text reflecting child choices with moderated automatic ripple edits
- Three-step pipeline: find changeable entities → generate parent questions → rewrite while preserving coherence
- Parents retain agency over which automatic span changes to accept

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Early model of **human–parent collaboration** with AI scaffolding—not replacing the child’s narrative decisions; aligns with relational agency and parent-mediated child–AI use.
