# UNICEF Guidance on AI for Children — Autism Robot Case

Three-week in-situ Pepper deployment in UK SEN school; maps nine UNICEF AI-for-children policy guidelines to participatory robot design.

## Key findings

- **Nine UNICEF guidelines** (well-being, inclusion, fairness, privacy, safety, transparency, stakeholder knowledge, AI literacy, enabling environment) grounded in UNCRC
- Participatory co-design with autistic pupils (13–16), teachers, parents, autistic academic; autistic voice central
- Shift from deficit-based social-skills training to **well-being** and emotional regulation (corridor, child-led, unstructured use)
- Robot behaviours (stories, dance, music, jokes, cuddling) co-designed; mood check-ins via school regulation materials
- Rights-based framing: children as agents with dignity, not passive welfare objects
- First systematic application of UNICEF AI guidance to **embodied** social robotics for autistic children

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Operationalises **child rights** and participatory design for vulnerable populations; contrasts protective-only AI with inclusion, transparency, and child-led interaction patterns.
