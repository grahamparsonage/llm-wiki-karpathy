## Definition

**Transformative agency**: children's ability to actively change their own matters, shared matters, or societal matters for a better and more just society—turning knowledge acquired in school or design into sustained action and change.

Not an individual trait; emerges collectively over time through interaction and consequence.

---

## Six manifestations (Haapasaari et al. / Engeström tradition)

1. Resisting
2. Criticising *(US sources: Criticizing)*
3. Explicating
4. Envisioning
5. Committing to action
6. Taking action

## Case projects

- **CEED** (Denmark) — emerging tech education; facial recognition fiction-based inquiry; teens 13–15
- **MAD** (Finland) — critical design/making against bullying; grades 3 and 6

Signs of transformative agency found across PD phases even when not an explicit project goal.

---

## Six supplemental PD aims for transformative agency

Beyond conventional CE/PD aims:

1. Design space for personal stance and motivation on the concrete matter
2. Explicate technology–culture links through everyday concerns
3. Scaffold empathetic yet critical pathfinding towards better practices
4. Explicate how construction may change practices and values
5. Support argument chains and community mobilisation for sustained change
6. Support children envisioning themselves as change agents

Mapped to framing, research, ideation, construction, argumentation, reflection.

---

## Limitations noted

- Nordic/Western epistemological roots; cultural translation needed for Global South
- Teachers' roles underexamined
- Not all children showed transformative agency in all activities
