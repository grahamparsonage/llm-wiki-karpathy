## Key findings

- Ready-made CA answers can limit **agency**; ZPD-inspired scaffolding via structured prompts improves question depth/coherence
- Parent–child homework scaffolds inform CA recipe design
- Recommendations: child profiles, caregiver-curated content, conversation trees
