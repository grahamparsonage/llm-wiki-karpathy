# Design Framework for Children's Agency (CHAI)

Participatory workshops introducing the **CHAI** framework to help designers reason about agency types, levels, and trade-offs in child–AI systems.

## Key findings

- Designers committed to ethical child–AI but struggle to operationalise **why agency matters** and how to implement it
- Agency often abstract/tick-box; ethics treated implicitly or retrospectively
- **CHAI framework**: individual, proxy, co-, and collective agency × graded support levels; four stages—assessment, mapping, application, reflection
- Workshops externalised implicit judgments into explicit **agency trade-offs**; added complexity but richer multidimensional reasoning
- Agency as foundational lens for transparency, accountability, fairness—not optional add-on
- Inspired by EbD-AI, RITEC/LEGO–UNICEF, rights-by-design
