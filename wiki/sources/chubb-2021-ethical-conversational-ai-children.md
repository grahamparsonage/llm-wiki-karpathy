## Ethical themes

- Privacy, safety, security, access for child users of IPAs (Alexa, Siri, etc.)
- Cognitive, social, linguistic development risks; reality vs fantasy in immersive AI
- Need to connect high-level ethics guidelines to **practical creative-industry** implementation
- Participatory child–computer interaction approaches for empowerment

---

## Technical stack noted

ASR, NLP, deep learning — ethical mapping for acoustic and data-driven models in a pilot meta-story CAI.

---

## Relevance to child–AI interaction

Frames **trust, bias, responsibility, power** in voice-based child–AI; urgent call for developer-facing ethics in storytelling CAI.
