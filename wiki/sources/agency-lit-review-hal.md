# Agency in Child–AI Interaction (Voysey et al.)

Rapid systematic review of 25 HCI studies on how children's agency is conceptualised, studied, and supported in child–AI interaction.

## Core definition used

Agency: *the ability to act intentionally to make decisions and enact them in order to achieve an outcome.* Authors note agency, autonomy, and empowerment are often used interchangeably in the corpus unless deliberately distinguished.

---

## Research questions and headline findings

| RQ | Finding |
|---|---|
| RQ1: Conceptualisation | Agency rarely explicitly defined; varies as innate possession, threatened capacity, or developable skill |
| RQ2: Evidence | Observed via choice-making, control expressions, critique/redesign; methods mostly inductive or incidental |
| RQ3: Enablers/constraints | Epistemic (literacy vs opacity), interactional design (choice vs displacement), social context (participation vs power), motivational (self-efficacy vs resignation) |
| RQ4: Recommendations | Control, choice, scaffolded literacy, active participation—for designers, researchers, policymakers, educators |

---

## Three forms of observed agency

1. **Agency over self** — planning, self-regulation (e.g., Coco's Videos playlist planning)
2. **Agency over system** — rejecting, adapting, pre-empting AI outputs (e.g., Scratch Copilot)
3. **Agency over environment** — critique and speculative redesign of socio-technical arrangements

---

## Design recommendation clusters (RQ4)

- **Control** — ultimate decision with child; configurable safety/moderation
- **Choice** — meaningful, understandable options; avoid choice overload
- **Scaffolded literacy** — understand limitations and data flows
- **Active participation** — children in design and governance, not only use

---

## Key arguments for the wiki

- Aligns with [[bennett-chi23-hci-agency-autonomy]]: agency remains an ill-defined umbrella in HCI
- Advocates **conceptual clarity** per study context, not one universal definition
- Balances **positive liberty** (enabling conditions) vs **negative liberty** (removing barriers); warns against zero-sum child-vs-system agency
- Proposes a **[[relational-agency-lens]]** — agency distributed across peers, families, educators, institutions
- Gap: transformative/critical agency mostly in speculative workshops, weak link to sustained praxis
- Next step: structured evaluation methods targeting specific agency forms
