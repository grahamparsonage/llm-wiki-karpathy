## What This Wiki Covers

A technical-writer knowledge base for documenting and communicating research on:

- How **agency**, **autonomy**, and **empowerment** are defined (and muddled) in HCI
- Empirical child–AI interaction studies (recommenders, copilots, voice agents, data platforms)
- **Computational Empowerment** as a CCI educational and participatory design paradigm
- **Transformative agency** as the maturation path from critique to sustained change
- Design and documentation implications for child-facing AI systems

Primary anchor source: [[agency-lit-review-hal]] (Voysey et al., 2026 preprint).

## Key Themes

| Theme | Summary |
|---|---|
| Conceptual ambiguity | HCI treats agency/autonomy as umbrella terms; child–AI papers rarely define them |
| Plural agency | Self-regulation, system control, environmental critique, transformative change |
| Design enablers | Literacy, meaningful choice, participation, balanced automation |
| Relationality | Agency is distributed across children, adults, institutions—not solo UX |
| CE maturation | From computational thinking → critical empowerment → transformative action |
| Evaluation gap | Need instruments per agency form; move beyond incidental mentions |
